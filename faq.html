General Questions

Q1: How do I register on StudentHub Portal?

Ans: Go to the Register page from the top navigation bar and fill in your registration details.


Q2: What should I use as my username during login?

Ans: Students can use their official Enrollment Number as their username.


Q3: Where can I check upcoming campus events?

Ans: You can check all upcoming activities and dates on the Events page.


Q4: How can I contact support?

Ans: Visit the Contact page to view our campus address, email, and phone contact info.
