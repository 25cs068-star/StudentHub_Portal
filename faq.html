General Questions


Q1: How do I register on StudentHub Portal?

Go to the Register page from the top navigation bar and fill in your registration details.

Q2: What should I use as my username during login?

Students can use their official Enrollment Number as their username.

Q3: Where can I check upcoming campus events?

You can check all upcoming activities and dates on the Events page.

Q4: How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page to view our campus address, email, and phone contact info.
