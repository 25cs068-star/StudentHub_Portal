About StudentHub

StudentHub Portal is a centralized platform designed to simplify academic and campus life for both students and faculty members. It provides quick access to schedules, notices, event registrations, and performance records.


Our Vision and Mission

  • Our Vision

    To create a digitally connected campus environment where every student has seamless access to academic resources and opportunities.


  • Our Mission

    To bridge the communication gap between administrators and students through a clean, fast, and accessible web portal.


User Roles in StudentHub

Student Role: View attendance, check exam notices, register for events, and manage personal profile.

Admin Role: Manage student accounts, publish new campus events, and handle institutional announcements.
